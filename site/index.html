Questions

Before you ask.

Is it free?

Yes. Otakase is open source under the GPL-3.0, with no ads, no accounts of its own and nothing to pay for.

Does Otakase host any video?

No. It is a player front end: it finds episodes that are already public on the web and plays them. Nothing is uploaded or stored by the project.

Do I need an AniList or MyAnimeList account?

No. Without one your progress stays on the device. You can sign in to AniList, MyAnimeList or both whenever you like.

What else do I need?

Nothing. The app brings its own player on every system.

Can I keep watching on another device?

Yes. With AniList or MyAnimeList, Otakase keeps the minute you stopped at with your list, so another device picks up mid-episode.

What happened to the command-line otakase?

It is being retired in favour of the app; v26.7.0 is the last release that carries it. Your list and watch positions carry over by themselves.

Install in a minute.

GET THE APK →

One app for phones and Android TV, Android 7.0 or newer. Android asks once to allow installing apps from your browser or file manager. Later updates install from More > About & updates.

GET THE INSTALLER →

Installs for your user only, no administrator needed, and adds Otakase to the Start menu.

GET THE APP →

Unzip and move Otakase to Applications; it runs on Apple Silicon and Intel. It is not signed by Apple yet, so the first time, right-click it and choose Open.

GET THE APPIMAGE →

chmod +x otakase-app-linux-x86_64.AppImage
./otakase-app-linux-x86_64.AppImage

Runs on any x86_64 distribution, with its player inside. Start it with --launcher for a small keyboard menu you can bind to a shortcut.
