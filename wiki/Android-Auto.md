# Android Auto

Otakase can play episodes on your car's screen through Android Auto while
the car is parked. It needs only the Otakase app on your phone. Video pauses
as soon as the car reports that it is moving.

Android Auto only lists apps from the Play Store unless you turn on its
developer setting for other apps. You do this once.

## Set up the phone

1. Install the Otakase APK.
2. Open the Android Auto settings: **Settings > Connected devices >
   Connection preferences > Android Auto** (on some phones search the
   settings for "Android Auto").
3. Scroll to the bottom and tap **Version** about ten times, then allow
   developer settings.
4. Open the three-dot menu at the top, pick **Developer settings** and turn
   on **Unknown sources**.
5. Back in the Android Auto settings, open **Customize launcher** and check
   that Otakase is ticked.
6. Open Otakase on the phone once, so your Continue watching list reaches
   the car.

## In the car

1. Connect the phone and open **Otakase** from the Android Auto app list.
2. The first time, the phone asks to let Otakase read the car's speed.
   Allow it: that is how video pauses when you drive off.
3. Pick an episode:
   - tap the list button on the car screen for **Continue watching**, or
   - play an episode on the phone, tap the cast button and choose
     **Car screen**.

The car screen has play/pause, back 10 seconds, forward 30 seconds and the
list. Tapping the picture also plays or pauses. The phone keeps showing
the player and its controls, like when casting to a TV. **Stop casting**
on the phone, or unplugging the phone, brings the episode back to the
phone at the same spot.

## Good to know

- Video only plays while the car is parked. A car that does not share its
  speed with Android Auto is not blocked, so stay parked.
- Leave Otakase open on the phone (in the background is fine): it fetches
  the stream while the car plays it.
- Films and series are not in the car's list yet. Cast them from the phone
  player instead.
- If Otakase is missing from the car's apps, check **Unknown sources**
  again, then unplug and plug the phone back in.
